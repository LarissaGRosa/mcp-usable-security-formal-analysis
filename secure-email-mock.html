Secure email — interactive mock

A working simulation of the two secure-send methods from the generic guide: OpenPGP end-to-end and password-based. Alex sends, Blake receives. The crypto is mocked (no real keys leave the page) so you can watch where each secret has to travel — including the out-of-band channel that both methods depend on.
